## 3. Logo Anatomy & Geometric Structure

### Visual Blueprint
```
                 [ Electric Sky Cyan #7CE0F9 ]
            ( * * * * * )               ( * * * * * )
         ( *             * )         ( *             * )
        ( *                 *       *                 * )
       ( *      LEFT         * (●) *       RIGHT       * )
        ( *     LOOP          (===)        LOOP       * )
         ( *                 *       *                 * )
            ( * * * * * * * )         ( * * * * * * * )
    [ Soft Periwinkle #BCD6F7 ]       [ Crimson Magenta #D83F70 ]
```

### Detailed Structural Elements:
1. **The Horizontal Infinity Symbol ($\infty$)**:
   - Continuous Möbius-style figure-8 ribbon symbolizing the decentralized compute and orthogonal persistence of the Internet Computer protocol.
   - Symmetrical left and right elliptical loops meeting at a central crossover node.
   - Fluid bevels with dynamic thickness: tapered inner waist expanding gracefully into rounded outer loops.

2. **The Central Human Silhouette (Person Avatar)**:
   - Positioned **directly at the central convergence point** within the aperture of the infinity loop.
   - Composed of two minimalist vector shapes:
     - **Head**: A crisp, solid circular dot centered above the torso.
     - **Shoulders / Body**: A smooth, curved arch contouring the upper torso.
   - Rendered in solid silhouette contrast (`#000000` / `#0E1118` against colored ribbons; clean inverted white `#FFFFFF` on dark backgrounds).
   - **Symbolic Meaning**: Putting the human user and sovereign username identity at the core of decentralized finance.

3. **Color Flow & Gradient Direction**:
   - **Main Ribbon**: Starts at top-left, cuts diagonally across the center in Crimson Magenta (`#D83F70`).
   - **Upper Loop**: Flows smoothly into Electric Sky Cyan (`#7CE0F9`), creating high-contrast visual clarity.
   - **Lower Loop**: Transitions back via Soft Periwinkle (`#BCD6F7`) before merging into the primary ribbon.
